What is the "landing page"?

You said "landing page for a progressive webapp" — which structure do you mean?

A

The map IS the app (like outletbuddy)

You open the app and land directly on the interactive map with the sidebar. No separate hero/marketing page — the experience starts immediately. This matches the reference images exactly.

B

Hero landing page → then the map

A marketing splash screen first (app name, tagline, "Start" button) that transitions into the map. Good for a hackathon demo where you need a moment to introduce CommuteSync before jumping into the data.

C

Scrollable landing page with embedded map

A multi-section page: hero section → how it works → live map preview → stats. Like a product homepage. The map is one section, not the whole screen.
